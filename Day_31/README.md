# Day 31 — RAG Evaluation Metrics

## Objective

Learn how to measure retrieval quality and evaluate answers generated by a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system.

- Retrieval evaluation

- F1 score

## Notebook

`31_rag_evaluation_metrics.ipynb`

## Key Formulas

### Precision

Relevant retrieved chunks / Total retrieved chunks

### Recall

Relevant retrieved chunks / Total relevant chunks

### F1 Score

2 × (Precision × Recall) / (Precision + Recall)

##

## Important Lessons

- Precision measures retrieval accuracy.
- Recall measures how much relevant information was found.
- F1 balances precision and recall.
- Faithfulness measures whether answers are supported by retrieved context.
- Correctness and faithfulness are different.
- Simple keyword checks are educational approximations, not reliable semantic evaluation.
